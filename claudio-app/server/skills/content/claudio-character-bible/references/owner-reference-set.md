# The owner's reference set, decoded

These pieces define "on-brand Claudio content". Every prompting skill in this library aims to recreate their look.
Files live in the site repo at `client/public/claudio/media/`. Read this when you need to match one of them shot for shot.

## Scenes (vertical 9:16 posters, each with a `-sq` 1:1 crop and a blurred backdrop)

Each scene is a still poster today; a silent 8–14 s loop with the same framing goes in as `<name>.mp4` (9:16) and
`<name>-sq.mp4` (1:1) when it is made.

### `room` — talking to camera at home
- **Frame:** front-camera phone at arm's length, eye level, head and torso to mid-waist, his extended arm entering from
  the bottom left.
- **Set:** plain white walls and ceiling, an open doorway behind, trailing pothos and potted plants at both sides. Flat,
  soft overcast daylight; no hard shadows.
- **Look:** signature olive t-shirt.
- **Beats (for the loop):** wide open laugh to lens → glance off to the side, talking → big smile, small sway → head
  tilt back, laughing. Continuous handheld micro-shake.

### `club` — at a DJ set
- **Frame:** medium shot from crowd height, his figure from the hips up, slightly turned; a DJ (generic man in a dark
  t-shirt) behind him at the decks.
- **Set:** DJ booth with mixers/CDJs, a crowd with raised arms and phones, blue-violet and magenta haze.
- **Look:** signature olive t-shirt, dark rectangular sunglasses, silver chain bracelet.
- **Beats (for the loop):** sway and laugh → one arm up on the beat → turns his head to the DJ, laughs. Light flickers on the beat.

### `lounge` — sunglasses in a lamp-lit vintage lounge
- **Frame:** medium shot, waist-up, a slow push-in to chest-up.
- **Set:** butter-yellow damask wallpaper, framed red-flower botanical prints, a brass lamp with a pleated cream shade at
  left, a dark wood dresser he leans back on. Warm tungsten key from the lamp.
- **Look:** signature olive t-shirt, dark rectangular sunglasses.
- **Beats (for the loop):** leaning, calm half-smile in sunglasses → slides the sunglasses down and looks over them.

### `rain` — running selfie in the rain, warehouse lot
- **Frame:** arm's-length phone, wide 24 mm look from slightly above, his extended arm and chunky silver chain bracelet
  dominating the foreground, rain visible against the wet ground.
- **Set:** empty warehouse/loading-dock lot, wet asphalt, roll-up doors, yellow bollards, heavy overcast sky.
- **Look:** signature olive t-shirt soaked by the rain, red-and-green tartan trousers, wet head.
- **Beats (for the loop):** running, laughing with his mouth wide open → spins a little, camera swings → laughs harder.

## What they have in common

- Real-camera feel: phone UGC or 35–85 mm photo language, never "digital art".
- Warm, slightly lifted grade; cool blue-violet only in club/tech scenes.
- One clear action per scene, no cuts inside a loop.
- He is the only sharp subject; background people are anonymous and generic.
- Wordmarks on banners are designed type — add them in edit with a licensed font rather than relying on model text.
